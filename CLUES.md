# Letterbox clues we steal (not new crypto)

Dartmoor / US letterbox methods:
- Bearing + paces from a mark (165°, 10 P)
- Shorthand: RH LH HP VCR GR P
- Cryptic / anagram / crossword flavor
- WOM (word of mouth) and secret-box — never printed
- Cuckoo clue — moves house
- Catalog vs 100 Club vs secret meet

We do **not** invent a field cipher. 100s = catalog clue. 400s = secret-box. Host spoken line = WOM.

Moor flavor for London 400s only: ponies, ravens, pixy-led (wrong street / coat inside-out as a joke teach), wisht-hounds as weather not a faction. Baskervilles is already on that moor. Do not build a pixie game.
